# Code of conduct

moviepix follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, criticize ideas rather than people, and
do not harass anyone. Report problems by opening a confidential
[security advisory](https://github.com/Hoopakid/moviepix/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.
